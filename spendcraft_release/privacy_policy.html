SpendCraft Privacy Policy

By Pratik Jain · Last updated: 30 September 2026

SpendCraft is an expense tracker for Android that works entirely on your phone. There is no account and no sign-up, the app has no internet access, and we never receive, see or store any of your data.

1. Data we collect

None. SpendCraft does not collect, transmit or share any personal or usage data.

Everything you enter — transactions, amounts, notes, categories, budgets and settings — is saved only in the app's private storage on your device. The app does not request the Android internet permission, so it cannot send data anywhere. It contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting software.

2. How your data is used

Your data is used only on your device, to show your balance, charts and budgets. We don't use it for anything else, because we never have access to it.

3. Sharing

We don't share or sell any data. If you choose Export to CSV, the app creates a file on your device and hands it to the Android share menu; where it goes next is entirely your choice. Tapping Privacy policy in the app opens this page in your browser app.

4. Storage & security

  • Data is stored in SpendCraft's private app storage, which other apps can't read.
  • Your data stays on this device. It is not backed up or synced by SpendCraft, so keep an exported CSV if you want a copy.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data.

5. Deleting your data

  • Delete individual transactions by swiping them away.
  • Delete everything with Settings → Clear all data, or by uninstalling the app.

Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

6. Children

SpendCraft is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no data from anyone.

7. Changes to this policy

If a future version of SpendCraft changes how data is handled — for example an optional paid cloud-backup feature — we'll update this page and its "Last updated" date before that version is released, and describe the change in the release notes.
